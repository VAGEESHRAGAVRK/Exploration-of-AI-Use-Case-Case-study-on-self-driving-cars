EXPLORATION OF AI USE CASE: CASE STUDY ON SELF-DRIVING CARS
============================================================

AIM
---

To study the application of Artificial Intelligence in self-driving cars
and understand how technologies such as Machine Learning, Computer Vision,
Deep Learning, Sensor Fusion, Decision Making, and Control Systems are
used to enable autonomous driving.


THEORY
------

Self-driving cars, also called autonomous vehicles, use Artificial
Intelligence to understand their surroundings, make decisions, and
control the vehicle with minimal human intervention.

The major AI technologies used in self-driving cars are:

1. Computer Vision
   Used to identify vehicles, pedestrians, traffic lights, road signs,
   lane markings, and other objects.

2. Machine Learning
   Helps the vehicle learn patterns from large amounts of driving data.

3. Deep Learning
   Neural networks are used for object detection, lane detection,
   traffic-sign recognition, and scene understanding.

4. Sensor Fusion
   Combines information from cameras, LiDAR, radar, GPS, and other
   sensors to understand the environment.

5. Path Planning
   Determines a safe path for the vehicle based on road conditions,
   obstacles, traffic, and destination.

6. Decision Making
   Determines actions such as braking, accelerating, stopping,
   and changing lanes.

7. Control Systems
   Converts decisions into commands for steering, braking, and
   acceleration.

WORKING STEPS
-------------

1. Environment Sensing

   The vehicle uses cameras, radar, LiDAR, GPS, and other sensors to
   collect information about the surrounding environment.

2. Object Detection

   AI algorithms identify objects such as cars, motorcycles,
   pedestrians, traffic signs, traffic lights, road boundaries,
   and lane markings.

3. Sensor Fusion

   Information from different sensors is combined to obtain a more
   accurate understanding of the environment.

4. Scene Understanding

   Computer Vision and Deep Learning algorithms analyze the road
   environment and identify important objects and road conditions.

5. Decision Making

   The AI system decides what action should be taken.

   Example:

   Pedestrian Detected
          |
          v
      Reduce Speed
          |
          v
     Check Distance
          |
          v
      Stop Vehicle

6. Path Planning

   The system calculates a safe path while considering traffic,
   obstacles, road conditions, and other vehicles.

7. Vehicle Control

   The final decisions are converted into commands for the steering,
   accelerator, and braking systems.

ADVANTAGES
----------

1. Reduces human driving workload.
2. Can improve road safety when properly designed and tested.
3. Provides assistance to people with limited mobility.
4. Can optimize driving routes.
5. Provides continuous monitoring of the environment.
6. Can improve traffic-flow efficiency.
7. Reduces dependence on manual driving.
8. Can react quickly to detected obstacles.


LIMITATIONS
-----------

1. AI systems can make incorrect decisions in unusual situations.
2. Heavy rain, fog, or poor visibility can affect sensors.
3. Complex traffic environments are difficult to interpret.
4. High-quality sensors and computing systems can be expensive.
5. Cybersecurity is an important concern.
6. Autonomous systems require extensive testing.
7. Human supervision may be required depending on the automation level.


RESULT
------

The application of Artificial Intelligence in self-driving cars was
studied successfully. The roles of Computer Vision, Machine Learning,
Deep Learning, Sensor Fusion, Path Planning, Decision Making, and
Control Systems in autonomous driving were understood.

Self-driving cars demonstrate how AI can combine information from
multiple sensors, understand the driving environment, make decisions,
and control a vehicle to perform driving tasks autonomously.


CONCLUSION
----------

Self-driving cars are an important real-world application of Artificial
Intelligence. By combining Computer Vision, Machine Learning, Deep
Learning, Sensor Fusion, and Decision-Making algorithms, autonomous
vehicles can perceive their surroundings and perform driving tasks.

The case study helped in understanding how AI technologies are applied
in autonomous vehicles to improve driving assistance, automation,
safety, and transportation efficiency.
